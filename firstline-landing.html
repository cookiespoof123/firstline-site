faq

Questions we get asked a lot

Will it feel robotic to my customers?

No — the responses are written to read like a real, brief text from your business, not a chatbot script. Most customers never realise the first reply was automatic; they just notice they heard back fast.

What if it books the wrong kind of job?

The qualifying questions are tailored to your business before launch, and anything unclear gets flagged to you rather than auto-booked. You stay in control of what actually lands on your calendar.

How long does setup take?

Most businesses are fully live within 5 business days of signing up, including your website, review requests, and text-back system.

Am I locked into a contract?

No long-term contract on any plan — you can cancel anytime. We'd rather earn the renewal every month than lock you in.

Do I need to change my phone number or website?

No. Firstline works with your existing number and site — we connect to what you already have rather than asking you to switch anything.
